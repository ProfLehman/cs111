# Project_01 Data Collection Design and Microsoft Forms
## Sample Project

**Project Title:**
Why Computer Science Students Choose Huntington University

**What I want to investigate:**
I want to investigate the factors that influence Computer Science students' decisions to attend Huntington University and why they choose Computer Science. In particular, I want to compare the importance of athletics with the importance of Huntington's Computer Science program in students' decisions to attend the university.

**Group I plan to survey:**
Current Huntington University Computer Science majors and minors. I will distribute the survey through several Computer Science courses and other appropriate channels in order to collect at least 25 valid responses.

**Prediction:**
I predict that among Computer Science students who participate in intercollegiate athletics, athletics will have a higher average importance rating than Huntington's Computer Science program in their decision to attend Huntington University.

**Microsoft Form Questions:**

1. **Approximately how many miles is Huntington University from your hometown?**

   * Text response restricted to numbers

2. **Approximately how many colleges or universities did you seriously consider before choosing Huntington University?**

   * Text response restricted to numbers

3. **Do you currently (or have you ever) participate in intercollegiate athletics at Huntington University?**

   * Yes
   * No

4. **Which factor was MOST important in your decision to attend Huntington University?**  Restrict to one choice.

   * Academic program or major
   * Athletics
   * Location / close to home
   * Financial aid / cost
   * Christian environment
   * Campus atmosphere
   * Family or friends
   * Size of the university
   * Other

5. **Which factors influenced your decision to attend Huntington University? Select all that apply.**

   * Academic program or major
   * Athletics
   * Location / close to home
   * Financial aid / cost
   * Christian environment
   * Campus atmosphere
   * Family or friends
   * Size of the university
   * Other

6. **How important were athletics in your decision to attend Huntington University?**

   * 1 = Not important
   * 2
   * 3
   * 4
   * 5 = Very important

7. **How important was Huntington University's Computer Science program in your decision to attend Huntington University?**

   * 1 = Not important
   * 2
   * 3
   * 4
   * 5 = Very important

8. **What was the PRIMARY reason you chose Computer Science?** Pick one.

   * Interest in computers or technology
   * Interest in programming
   * Career opportunities
   * Expected salary or earning potential
   * Previous programming or computing experience
   * Recommendation from a teacher, family member, or friend
   * I developed an interest in Computer Science after coming to Huntington
   * Other
